Our Metal Fabrication Process — Orlando, FL

At ProCut & Coat, we follow a streamlined, in-house metal fabrication process that moves projects from raw sheet metal to finished, powder-coated parts — all within our Orlando facility. By keeping laser cutting, forming, and finishing under one roof, we reduce delays and maintain full control over quality and scheduling.

1. Project Intake & Technical Review

Every project begins with a clear review of drawings, quantities, material specifications, and finish requirements. One technical team evaluates the scope to confirm manufacturability, timeline, and production flow before work begins.

For early-stage projects, we can assist with design refinement or CAD file preparation to ensure parts are production-ready.

2. Precision Laser Cutting

Approved parts move to precision laser cutting. We cut steel, aluminum, and other common fabrication metals accurately to specification, preparing components for forming or finishing.

Because cutting is completed in-house, parts transition directly to the next stage without outside handoffs.

3. Sheet-Metal Bending & Forming

After cutting, components are formed to required angles and dimensions using consistent, repeatable processes. This step ensures structural integrity and proper fit for brackets, panels, enclosures, and other fabricated parts.

4. Powder Coating & Controlled Curing

When finishing is required, parts move to our in-house powder coating system. Coatings are applied and cured under controlled conditions to create durable, professional-grade finishes suitable for functional and aesthetic applications.

5. Quality Check & Scheduled Delivery

Before completion, parts undergo a final inspection to verify accuracy, finish quality, and order consistency. Clear scheduling and communication ensure predictable turnaround and delivery timelines.

Because every stage — laser cutting, sheet-metal forming, and powder coating — happens in one Orlando facility, we minimize production gaps, reduce scheduling friction, and maintain consistent quality from raw metal to finished parts.
